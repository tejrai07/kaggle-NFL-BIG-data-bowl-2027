## Judging questions to design for
- Football: usable week to week? accounts for football complexity? unique ideas?
- Data science: correct? claims backed by data? appropriate models? innovative?
- Writeup: easy to follow? motivation clearly defined?
- Visualization: accessible, accurate, innovative?
Organizers encourage a narrow focus (one movement aspect, position group or drill).
