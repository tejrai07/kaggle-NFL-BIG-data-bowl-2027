## 3. Measurement reliability, sprint mechanics, tracking error
- **COD tests, stopwatch.** A systematic review of COD tests in adolescent female soccer players reports ICC 0.72-0.99 across 11 tests (Pardos-Mainer et al. 2020, J Sports Med Phys Fitness) **[P]**; a study of five COD tests (n=44 physical-education students) reports ICC 0.88-0.95 (DOI 10.1111/sms.12019) **[P]**; 3-cone test versions show moderate-high ICC 0.79 and SEM about 0.09-0.10 s in non-athletic men (J Sports Sci Med) **[P]**. *Implication:* integrated stopwatch times are reliable. Populations differ from NFL prospects, so use as context only.
- **Instantaneous kinematics from 10 Hz position data.** Akenhead et al. 2014, J Sci Med Sport 17(5):562-566 **[P]**: validity and inter-unit reliability of 10 Hz GPS velocity worsen as acceleration rises (SEE 0.12 -> 0.32 m/s from low to >4 m/s^2). Bataller-Cervero et al. 2019 (J Hum Kinet, DOI 10.2478/hukin-2018-0084) **[P]**: 10 Hz GPS instantaneous speed vs radar r=0.98. *Caveat:* these are GPS devices, not the NFL's RFID system; the NFL tracking system's own error profile was not verified here. The pattern (speed is measured well, peak acceleration/braking less so) agrees with our data but does not prove the cause.
- **Sprint model.** The mono-exponential velocity-time model V(t)=Vmax(1-exp(-t/tau)) is established; Samozino et al. 2016, Scand J Med Sci Sports 26(6):648-658 **[P]** report SEM between trials <5% in sprinters under controlled conditions. *Implication:* our use of it is not novel; our poor tau reliability (0.36) reflects Combine clips/tracking, not the model's validity per se.

## 4. Small-sample prediction and incremental value
- Riley et al. 2019, Stat Med (Part I, continuous outcomes) **[P]**: minimum n should give shrinkage >=0.9, apparent-adjusted R^2 difference <=0.05 and precise residual SD/intercept. Riley et al. 2020, BMJ 368:m441 **[P]** (tutorial). Riley et al. 2021, J Clin Epidemiol 132:88-96 **[P]**: penalization/shrinkage can be unreliable when the sample is small. *Applied by us (calculation, not run through the pmsampsize package):* criterion (i) for 15 predictors and R^2=0.2 needs about 600 players; our ~60 per group are far below that for model *development*. This is why we frame conclusions as bounds on incremental R^2 (CI) and why ridge/nested lambda results are interpreted cautiously.
- I did **not** search/verify literature on net reclassification or incremental-validity indices beyond the above; no claim is made from that body of work.
